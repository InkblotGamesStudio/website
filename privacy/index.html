Privacy

What this site collects.

Short version: your email address if you choose to subscribe, and anonymous visit counts. No advertising, no tracking cookies, nothing sold or shared.

Mailing list

If you subscribe, your email address is sent to Kit, the service that stores the list and sends the emails. It is used only to send updates about Inkblot Games and its games. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from future emails. To have your address deleted entirely, email the address below.

Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, pages viewed, and referring sites. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not track you across other sites.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Like any web server, both receive your IP address when your browser requests files from them. See the privacy policies of GitHub and Google Fonts.

Links to other sites

Links to YouTube, X, and Discord open those sites, which have their own privacy practices. Nothing from them is loaded on this site until you click.
